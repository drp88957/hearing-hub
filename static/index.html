Every conversation is a campfire. Take your seat.

For as long as humans have lived in groups, belonging has meant following the talk around the fire. Hearing loss pushes people to the edge of the circle. Hearing Hub, made by someone who lives with hearing loss, helps you explain how you hear, skip the dreaded phone call, pick the best seat and keep up with the group.

You are at the edge of the circle.

Upload your hearing report

Add your audiogram or hearing report, plus any other pictures that help, such as your hearing device, an ENT note or a medical certificate. The app reads them and fills in your numbers below. The Phone calls and Places tabs use what it finds, so upload your report here first.

    Used in your share card and in every tool. Write one fact per line. Kept only while this tab is open.

    Files are read once and are not saved on the server. Names, addresses and ID numbers are never copied out. Always check the numbers against your original report.
